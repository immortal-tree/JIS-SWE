JISJudiciary Information System

The court register, hearings and case records in one place.

Registrars run the docket. Judges and lawyers search the record of past cases.

  • Register cases and issue CINs
  • Schedule hearings in vacant court slots
  • Record adjournments, proceedings and judgments
  • Search closed cases by keyword

Access is limited to court staff, judges and registered lawyers. Every case view by a lawyer is recorded.
